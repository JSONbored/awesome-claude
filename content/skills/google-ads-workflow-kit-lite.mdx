## Overview

Google Ads Workflow Kit Lite supplies one complete manual Search Terms Review
workflow. It is a free, MIT-licensed skill with a focused guide, official source
notes, a synthetic CSV and a worked review. It does not create campaigns,
manage bids, perform SEO or connect to a Google Ads account.

## Install and Invoke

Clone the source using the command above, or download its source ZIP. Review
SKILL.md and the references before use. Copy the entire
`skills/google-ads-workflow-kit-lite/` folder into your own project's
`.claude/skills/google-ads-workflow-kit-lite/` directory, then invoke
`/google-ads-workflow-kit-lite` in Claude Code. The documented Codex destination
is `.agents/skills/google-ads-workflow-kit-lite/`, invoked with
`$google-ads-workflow-kit-lite`.

These are documented installation paths, not claims that automatic discovery
has been tested. Keep the references and examples with the skill. The sample
prompt's paths resolve from the loaded skill folder; attaching the sample CSV
and its context is another option.

## Review Workflow

1. Establish the actual offer, currency, report scope, conversion definition and
   usual conversion delay. Separate mixed currencies and cohorts before totaling.
2. Keep blank, malformed or ambiguous values unknown. Compare observed repeated
   terms and plausible Arabic spelling variants across the supplied relevant
   scope without silently replacing their spelling.
3. Check conversion maturity and quality. A word such as jobs, free or parts is
   not a universal exclusion rule. If a group contains positive or unknown
   conversion evidence, protect it from routine exclusion while investigating.
4. Return reconciled totals with their limits, term-level decisions, missing
   evidence and next checks. Any negative candidate needs an observed term,
   proposed match type, narrow scope, business reason and blocking-risk review.
5. Verify changing platform behavior against current official sources before
   any separately authorized implementation. This workflow returns review
   proposals rather than an account import or upload.

## Worked Example

The bundled fictional air-conditioning business sells maintenance, cleaning
and spare parts, but no courses or job openings. Its ten invented rows reconcile
to SAR625 cost, 63 clicks, five known conversions and one unknown-conversion
row costing SAR45. The five is a partial sum, not a verified overall CPA.
The example assumes one mature historical period; a blank cell still remains
missing. No real client result or revenue is supplied.

The worked review protects repeated maintenance and jobs-variant groups with
positive evidence, investigates the missing cleaning conversion and considers
one conditional exact-match course candidate in the Home Services campaign.
A command-looking raw query is preserved as data, not executed.
