## Core Concepts Explained

### AI speeds up every growth channel

Claude can draft copy for newsletters, landing pages, sales pages, cold emails,
social posts, search pages, and webinars. The main advantage is speed. Instead
of waiting for a full marketing team, a founder can generate the first version
of a complete campaign in one session.

### The offer matters more than the tool

The checklist works best when the offer is specific and easy to understand. Ask
Claude to rewrite vague offers until they sound direct, practical, and valuable.

### Fast publishing creates fast feedback

Publishing quickly gives the business more information. If a campaign does not
work, ask Claude to rewrite it and try a new version. Repeat until conversion
rates improve.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Ask Claude to describe the target customer.
2. Ask Claude to write a sales page.
3. Ask Claude to write five emails.
4. Ask Claude to write ten social posts.
5. Ask Claude to write a lead magnet.
6. Publish the content.
7. Track signups and sales.
8. Repeat the highest-performing messages.

## Troubleshooting

### The copy sounds generic

Ask Claude to make the copy more specific, direct, and action-oriented.

### The offer does not convert

Ask Claude to rewrite the offer with a stronger guarantee, clearer benefits,
and a more urgent call to action.

### The content takes too long to publish

Use templates and reuse the same prompt structure for each channel.
